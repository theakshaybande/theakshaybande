## Featured Quant & HFT Engineering

### **[Regime-Conditioned Multivariate Motif Discovery](https://github.com/theakshaybande/Final_master_thesis)**
**Python · Financial Time Series · Matrix Profile · LoCoMotif · HMM**

MSc research benchmarking regime-agnostic and regime-conditioned motif discovery under financial nonstationarity, with volatility and latent-state conditioning across multiple asset classes.

### **[Machine Learning for Market Making](https://github.com/theakshaybande/ML-MarketMaking-XGBoost)**
**Python · XGBoost · Random Forest · Neural Networks · EUR/USD · Backtesting**

Built a short-horizon FX signal research pipeline with technical feature engineering, PCA, model comparison, signal generation, and backtesting on 5-minute EUR/USD data. The repository reports an XGBoost strategy result of **9.4% return with 6% market exposure** over its six-month test setup.

### **[Lock-Free-Oriented Limit Order Book](https://github.com/theakshaybande/lockfree-limit-order-book)**
**C++20 · Market Microstructure · Memory Pools · Cache-Aware Design**

Implemented a deterministic price-time-priority order book with direct order-ID indexing, a fixed-capacity memory pool, cache-line-aligned order storage, and no per-order heap allocation on the add/cancel/match path.

### **[Branchless Matching Engine](https://github.com/theakshaybande/branchless-matching-engine)**
**C++20 · Matching Engines · Microbenchmarking · Low-Latency Design**

Compared conventional branching with branchless-style matching logic over deterministic synthetic order flow. Includes correctness tests and a one-million-order-pair microbenchmark, with explicit discussion of branch prediction and measurement limitations.

### **[C++20 Market Data Pipeline](https://github.com/theakshaybande/cpp20-market-data-pipeline)**
**C++20 · Atomics · string_view · from_chars · CMake**

Market-data parsing and processing scaffold focused on low-overhead tokenization, normalization, aggregation, compile-time constraints, and concurrency-oriented design.

### **[Market Chaos Lab](https://github.com/theakshaybande/market-chaos-lab)**
**C++20 · Limit Order Book · Agent-Based Markets · Market Microstructure**

Order-driven market simulator where momentum, mean-reversion, and random trader archetypes interact through shared liquidity, producing emergent price behavior through an explicit limit-order-book matching process.

### **[HFT Reinforcement Learning Trader](https://github.com/theakshaybande/hft-rl-trader)**
**Python · PyTorch · Gymnasium · DQN · Limit Order Book**

Experimental reinforcement-learning environment for inventory management and quote placement in a stylized LOB market with spread, fees, PnL, and risk-aware reward design.
